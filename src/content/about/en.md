I like taking ideas all the way: designing a part, calculating it, making it and seeing it work. I've done it at work — redesigning welded sheet metal assemblies and solving failures with FEA in AUSA's R&D team — and in my own projects, like the RC sailboats that took **runner-up at the RC Sailing Barcelona race** two years in a row.

Running the ETSEIB digital fabrication lab taught me to think about how my designs will be made (DFM), which is why I'm as interested in mechanical design as in **process** and **quality** engineering.

Outside engineering, music and student associations have taught me teamwork and leadership through managing projects with many stakeholders.

## The challenge

Starting from paper drawings of an early 20th-century yacht, I had to rebuild its shape in 3D and adapt it so the model would be stable and sailable under radio control.

## Process

I digitised the cross-sections in **Rhino**, faired the curves and generated the frames for laser cutting. The hull was planked with basswood strips and sealed with resin.

## Result

A 1.1 m model, faithful to the original and stable enough to sail in moderate wind.

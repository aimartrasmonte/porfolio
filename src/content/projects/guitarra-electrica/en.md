## Goal

To design a fully parametric guitar, where the scale length, neck profile and body shape can be changed from a table of variables, and to build a working prototype.

## Design

The **SolidWorks** model is driven by a design table. I calculated the total string tension (≈ 450 N) and sized the truss rod so the maximum neck deflection stays below 0.3 mm.

![Neck and fretboard before gluing](./images/02.jpg)

## Manufacturing

Toolpaths were generated in **Fusion 360 CAM** and the body was milled from ash. Frets, electronics and finishing were done by hand.

## Results

- 1.8 mm action at the 12th fret with no fret buzz.
- Body milling time under 3 hours.

## Goal

To build a testing rig of up to 5 kN, for under €400, able to characterise 3D-printing materials following ISO 527.

## Design

The aluminium extrusion frame was sized for bending and buckling. The actuator is a ball screw driven by a stepper motor, and force is measured with an S-type load cell.

## Results

Stress–strain curves for PLA and PETG were within 5 % of the manufacturer's reference values.

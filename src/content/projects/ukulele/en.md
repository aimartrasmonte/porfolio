## Goal

To understand how soundboard bracing affects an instrument's sound, and to use simulation to make design decisions before building.

## Simulation

I modelled the soundboard with orthotropic spruce properties and computed its first vibration modes in **Ansys**. I compared three bracing layouts.

## Build and validation

After building the instrument, I measured its response with a microphone and **Audacity** (tap test). The frequencies of the first two modes were within 8 % of the simulation.

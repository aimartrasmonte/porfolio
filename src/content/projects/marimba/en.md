## Goal

To build a marimba where every bar sounds exactly at its intended note, by adjusting its shape before making it.

## Method

Each bar's frequency depends on its geometry. Using **modal FEA**, I computed the vibration modes and iteratively modified the geometry until each bar was in tune.
